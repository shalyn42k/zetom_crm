   claude — body rephrased from "{{ resolver_name }} {{ decision }} your
   review request." to a colon+label structure (same shape as the title
   line above): the old phrasing reused decision as a past-tense VERB,
   which only works because English approved/rejected are identical in
   adjective and verb form — Polish needs a different, gender-agreeing
   word for the verb ("zatwierdził"/"zatwierdziła") than for the label
   ("Zatwierdzone"), and resolver gender isn't tracked anywhere here, so a
   single label substitution can't produce a grammatically correct verb
   form in Polish. Restructuring around a label-only sentence sidesteps
   that instead of guessing a gender.
